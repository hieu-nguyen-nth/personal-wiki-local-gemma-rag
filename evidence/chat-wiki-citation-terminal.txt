K-means alternates two operations: assigning each example to its nearest centroid and moving each centroid to the mean of its assigned examples [2].

The process involves these steps:

1.  **Assign points to cluster centroids:** Assign each data point to its nearest centroid.
2.  **Recompute the centroid:** Recompute the centroid by taking the average of all the points assigned to it [1].

This process is repeated until it converges (no change in the centroid) [1].

**Suggestion:** Different initial centroids can produce different local optima, so the source recommends repeated random initialization and selecting the run with the lowest cost [2].
