# Product Leadership Archetypes

The original research distinguishes three product-leadership archetypes:

- **Operator:** strong at scaling teams, aligning organizations, removing execution barriers, and creating operational clarity.
- **Craftsperson:** strong at product definition, customer insight, user experience, strategy, and mentoring product managers.
- **Visionary:** oriented toward original direction and longer-term product possibilities.

People can combine archetypes. The useful question is not which label is universally best, but which strengths a situation needs and where an individual needs complementary partners or growth.
