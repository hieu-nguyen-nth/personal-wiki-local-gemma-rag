# AI-First Product Management

The source notes argue that AI changes product-management execution without removing the need for product judgment. AI can accelerate drafting, synthesis, updates, competitive monitoring, and prototyping, which moves attention toward customer research, strategy, decisions, and coordination.

One note distinguishes product research—learning what should be built through customers, data, and experiments—from product development, which turns that knowledge into a product or service. It also argues that visual specifications and prototypes can narrow communication gaps between product and engineering.
