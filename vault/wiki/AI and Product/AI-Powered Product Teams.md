# AI-Powered Product Teams

## Key Themes
*   Product work is changing, requiring a pivot with the wave.
*   AI is transforming product development faster than expected.
*   AI may collapse the traditional talent stack (Product - Tech - Design), leading to generalist specialists.
*   Product leaders must prepare for changes and focus on skills.

## Automating PM Work
The focus should shift to automating tasks to speed up delivery:
*   **Anti-todo list examples to automate:**
    *   Drafting documents.
    *   Getting feedback.
    *   Writing updates and summaries.
    *   Monitoring goals/OKRs and creating them.
    *   Tracking competitors.
    *   Preparing for interviews and synthesizing information.
    *   Telling customer stories.
    *   Making slides and explaining functionality.
*   **Approach to execution:** Focus on performance (automating 0 to 75% and fine-tuning to 85%) rather than striving for 100%.
*   **Value:** Freeing up time for users and creativity.
*   **Skill Development:** Use new time to acquire new skills.
*   **Leveraging AI:** Anyone can be a PM using AI for tasks like documentation and design.
*   **Impact:** Multiply impact by teaching the team.

## Future State of Product Leadership
*   **Talent Stack Collapse:** Jobs may collide, leading to a focus on one individual rather than the traditional triad (Product - Tech - Design with AI).
*   **Role Evolution:**
    *   The role may shift toward generalist specialists.
    *   Product, design, and engineering may converge within one person using AI-powered tools and agents.
    *   The role may evolve to combine inbound product manager vs. outbound product marketing.
    *   A lean group of senior product may remain, influencing rather than controlling design and engineering.
*   **Actionable Advice:** Experience and skills remain important; therefore, focus on skill development.
