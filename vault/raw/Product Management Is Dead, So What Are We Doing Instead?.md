- **Become an AI-powered PM**
	- Automate yourself to speed up delivery, look at the anti-todo list - things that you shouldn't do anymore
		- the anti-todo list
			- draft documents
			- getting feedback
			- writing updates and summary, next steps
			- monitoring goals/OKRs and coming up with them
			- keeping track of competitors
			- prep for interviews, synthesis information
			- tell customer stories
			- making slides and explain the product functionality
		- **think: i wish i don't have to do it**
		-  It's not about craftmanship (do every single things on your own tỉ mỉ, it's about performance (not try to do things from 0 to 100, but automate 0 to 75 and fine tune it to be 85+)
			- don't look at it as getting 100%, looking at how you can fet 70% and work your way up, faster than starting from 0
			- **value**: more time for user, for creativity 
	- use new time to Add new skills and do more
		- with AI, any people can be the PM using AI (docs, design, etc) -> people of different background eg tech, design are coming to product jobs, thinking about that's the future that i need to handle
	- Multiple your impact by teaching your team
- vision for the future - **AI will collapse the talent stack** 
	- -> jobs may collide and drive by one individual instead of the triad Product - Tech - Design with AI
	- Generalist specialist
